- 原有标注、撤销重做、橡皮擦、马赛克、文件编号、剪贴板、自动截图和中英文 OCR 回归。

Tests used separate settings and output directories. Production settings were not modified.

页面示例图为新版实际界面的 2× 渲染，不含实测桌面截图或个人配置。
Preview images are 2× renders with demonstration content, not captured personal desktop images.

限制 / Limits:
窗口吸附选择可见应用的边界，不还原被遮挡的窗口内容；不捕捉最小化窗口。
多屏混合 DPI、所有辅助浮层、不同机器 OCR 准确率和各类粘贴目标未穷尽测试。
简体中文、英语和混排识别通过；本机未安装繁体 OCR，未进行繁体准确率实测。
Window snapping selects bounds of visible applications; it does not restore occluded content or minimized windows.
Mixed-DPI multi-monitor setups, every overlay type, OCR accuracy on other machines and all paste destinations are not exhaustively tested.
Traditional Chinese OCR was not tested because its language pack is not installed here.
